The short version

Lulla plays ambient sleep sounds. It has no accounts and does not collect your name, email, location, contacts or microphone audio. It does not record sound.

What stays on your device

Lulla stores, on your device, how many free listening seconds you have used (in the device keychain or keystore) and your sound and timer choices. This is used to enforce a free listening period of three minutes before the paywall. Deleting the app may not reset the free-minute count on iOS, because it is stored in the keychain.

Subscriptions and RevenueCat

Lulla Premium is an auto-renewing subscription sold as product lulla_premium_monthly through the Apple App Store or Google Play. The price is shown in the app and on the store listing before you buy and is charged by Apple or Google. We never see your payment card details.

To confirm whether your subscription is active and to restore it, the app uses RevenueCat. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase status. We do not receive your name, payment card or email from the store through this service.

What we do not do

We do not sell your data, show third-party ads, or track you across other apps or websites. There is no analytics or crash-reporting SDK in the app.

Background audio

Lulla can keep playing when your screen is locked so sleep sounds and the timer can continue. It does not record audio.

Children

Lulla is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.
