Lulla - Sleep Sounds Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

The short version

Lulla plays ambient sleep sounds. We collect as little data as possible: no account, no name, no email, no location, no contacts, no audio recording.

What stays on your device

Lulla remembers how many free minutes you have used, and your sound and timer choices, on your device only.

Subscriptions

Lulla Premium is $4.99 per month or $41.99 per year (or the local equivalent) and renews automatically until cancelled. Payment is processed by Apple or Google; we never see your payment details. Our subscription provider, RevenueCat, receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase status so we can tell whether your subscription is active. Manage or cancel in your Apple ID or Google Play subscription settings at least 24 hours before renewal.

What we don’t do

We do not sell your data, show third-party ads, or track you across other apps or websites.

Background audio

Lulla keeps playing when your screen is locked so your sleep sounds and timer continue. It does not record audio.

Children

Lulla is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update this page and its date.
