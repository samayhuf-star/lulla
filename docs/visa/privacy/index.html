Krosz Visa Guide Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

The short version

Krosz Visa Guide helps you check visa requirements and photo rules. Version 1.0 has no account or login and does not collect, upload or share your personal data.

Visa information

Visa and document information is bundled inside the app and looked up on your device. Your searches, such as your passport country and destination, are not sent to us.

Photo checker

If you choose a photo to check, the app reads that one photo on your device to compare it with visa photo rules. The photo is not uploaded or stored by us. The app only gets the photo you pick.

Advertising and tracking

No ads, no analytics SDKs, and no tracking across other apps or websites.

Future versions

A later version may offer an optional way to share your name, email or phone so Krosz can help with your visa application. If so, we will ask first, explain how it is used, and update this policy before it ships.

Not legal advice

Visa rules change. The app is a guide only; always confirm with the official embassy or government source before you apply.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update this page and its date.
