## Processing spec (for the next step, after source approval)

Do this only after the list is approved. Do not start an EAS build, and do not replace files in the app repo until the processed masters are ready.

1. **Download** the original chosen file from each Freesound page (WAV/FLAC where available). Freesound requires a logged-in account for original downloads. Keep a local copy of this manifest beside the downloads.
2. **Inspect and trim** out handling noise, abrupt starts/stops, voices, music, traffic, or other off-character sounds. Prefer a stable interior section.
3. **Loop:** build a seamless crossfade loop of **60 to 120 seconds**. If the source is already longer, cut a clean 60–120 s bed and crossfade the ends (about 1–3 s). If the source is shorter than 60 s (Rain on Window, Rain on Puddle, and the Cave backup), repeat the bed and crossfade the joins so the loop does not click or breathe.
4. **Loudness:** normalize to about **-18 LUFS** (integrated), with true peak below **-1 dBTP**. Keep the relative character of rain, fire, thunder, and drips; do not squash transients with heavy limiting.
5. **Format for the app:** AAC / M4A, **128–192 kbps**, **44.1 kHz or 48 kHz**, stereo unless the source is truly mono (Night is two-channel mono; the noise WAV backups are mono). Downsample 96 kHz and 192 kHz sources to 48 kHz with a high-quality resampler.
6. **Keep filenames and display names** exactly as they are now (`rain.mp3` becomes `rain.m4a` only if the app wiring is updated in the same change; otherwise keep the current `*.mp3` filenames or match whatever `catalog.ts` will require).
7. **License log:** store this manifest with the processed files, including title, author, URL, and “Creative Commons 0” for each sound. Attribution is not required under CC0, but the log should still be kept.

## Coverage summary

- **25 of 25** current sounds have a verified CC0 chosen source and a CC0 backup.
- **No sound is left without a commercially usable match.** The weaker fits, still usable, are:
  - **Snow:** real snowy-day ambience, not audible snowflakes.
  - **Cave:** good real cave drips exist, but many popular “cave” tracks are designed or musical; the chosen and backup are actual caves.
  - **Fan:** the long pedestal-fan take is close-mic’d on the motor and may need EQ.
  - **White / pink / brown noise:** DSP-generated (Audacity), which is the correct source type; not field recordings and not AI.
- **Download blocker:** Freesound original downloads require an account. Nothing was downloaded in this step.
- **Not used:** Pixabay and Sonniss GDC were not needed, because Freesound CC0 covered every slot with a checked page URL.
