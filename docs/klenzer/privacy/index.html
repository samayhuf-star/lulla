Klenzer Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

The short version

Klenzer works entirely on your device. We do not collect, upload, sell or share your photos, videos or any personal data. There is no account, no analytics and no advertising.

What Klenzer accesses

With your permission, Klenzer reads your photo and video library on-device to detect duplicate or near-identical photos, screenshots, Live Photos and large videos, and to measure their file sizes. It also reads your device’s total and free storage to show it on the home screen.

Contacts, calendar and Secret Space

If you open these tools and allow access, Klenzer reads your contacts or calendar on-device to find duplicate contacts and events that ended long ago. Before merging contacts, Klenzer saves a backup file inside the app; you can copy it to Files or iCloud Drive from the share sheet. Deleted calendar events can’t be restored by Klenzer. Secret Space copies photos and videos you choose into Klenzer’s private storage on your device, locked behind Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode; Klenzer never sees your biometric data. Because that storage belongs to the app, deleting Klenzer deletes what is in Secret Space, so restore anything you want to keep first.

What leaves your device

Nothing. Scan results are kept in memory on your device and discarded when you close the app. Your photos, videos, contacts and calendar are never transmitted to us or to anyone else.

Purchases

Klenzer offers an optional one-time Lifetime purchase and, in some regions, an optional monthly subscription to unlock cleaning. Payment is handled entirely by Apple or Google; we never see your payment details, and we receive no personal information from the purchase.

What is stored on your device

Klenzer stores a few small preferences locally: whether you have finished the intro, the running total of space you have cleared, whether you turned reminders on, and (on iPhone) how much space is still waiting in Recently Deleted. Klenzer also keeps temporary files such as image thumbnails, which you can clear from the home screen. Deleting the app removes all of this.

Deleting photos

Klenzer only deletes items you select, and only after your phone’s own confirmation prompt. On iPhone, deleted items go to Recently Deleted, where you can restore them for up to 30 days; the space is freed once that album is emptied. iOS does not let apps empty it, so Klenzer offers a shortcut to Photos for you to do it. On Android, items you confirm are deleted by the system and Klenzer cannot restore them.

Reminders

If you turn them on, Klenzer schedules reminder notifications about once every 3 days that mention how full your storage is (up to 10 at a time, restarted whenever you open the app). They are created and delivered by your phone itself: no server is involved, nothing is sent to us, and no push token is collected. Turn them off any time in Klenzer Settings or your device Settings.

Permissions

Photo and video library access is the only permission Klenzer needs to scan. Contacts, calendar, Face ID and notification permissions are optional, and each is only requested when you open the tool that uses it or choose reminders. You can change or revoke either at any time in your device Settings; Klenzer will still open but cannot scan without photo access.

Children

Klenzer collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be shown here and in the store listing, with a new date.
