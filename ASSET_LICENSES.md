All 27 recorded originals are in (none pending). Processing for every recorded sound: trimmed to the steadiest window, EQ, seamless 5 s equal-power loop, -20 LUFS, true peak at or below -1 dBTP, AAC 160k. Some peaky sources (rain on tent, window and umbrella, thunder, creek, waterfall, fireplace, air conditioner, cave, farm) also get gentle compression; per-file settings are in `reports/lulla-b8/masters/master-results.json`. Farm's loop starts at 190 s of the original to stay clear of a loud close event at 184-187 s. Snow, Cave and Farm all ship from their first-choice sources (no backups used).

"Ah! vous dirai-je, maman" are public domain; the MIDI is written in-house in `tools/sound-pipeline/b8/generate.py` (no edition copied). Schubert Wiegenlied D 498 (1816) is public domain; its melody was transcribed in-house from the public-domain Mutopia Project typesetting (Mutopia-2007/10/03-1040, marked "Public Domain", https://www.mutopiaproject.org/ftp/SchubertF/D498/schubert_wiegen_lied/). The Brahms melody was checked note by note against the CC0 OpenScore Lieder edition (github.com/OpenScore/Lieder, lc5701612).

Womb (`womb.m4a`): own mix from Heartbeat #369017 by patobottos (CC0 1.0), slowed 8 percent and low-passed, looped on whole beats, over low-passed generated brown noise.
